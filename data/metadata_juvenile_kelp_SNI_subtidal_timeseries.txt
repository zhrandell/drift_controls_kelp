Metadata for the San Nicolas Island kelp and invertebrate data used to analyze juvenile Giant kelp. 

The biological variables are counts of individuals recorded within fixed 10 m x 2 m (20 m2) benthic band transects at permanent subtidal monitoring sites around San Nicolas Island, California. Scientific names below reflect currently accepted taxonomy; historical names are noted where relevant.

[, 1] - date
The date on which the transect survey was conducted.

[, 2] - PERIOD
A numeric identifier for the sampling period, as recorded in the source data.

[, 3] - SEASON
The season in which the survey was conducted (for example, "SPRING" or "FALL").

[, 4] - SITE
A numeric identifier for the permanent subtidal monitoring site. The study included six sites around San Nicolas Island.

[, 5] - ID
A unique transect identifier formed by combining the SITE and TRANSECT values (for example, "1_10R").

[, 6] - TRANSECT
The identifier assigned to the permanent 10 m x 2 m benthic band transect. Each site contained five permanent transects.

[, 7] - PatMin
The count of Patiria miniata (bat star) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 8] - MesFra
The count of Mesocentrotus franciscanus (red sea urchin) individuals recorded on the transect. This species was formerly classified as Strongylocentrotus franciscanus.

[, 9] - StrPur
The count of Strongylocentrotus purpuratus (purple sea urchin) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 10] - MegUnd
The count of Megastraea undosa (wavy turban snail) individuals recorded on the transect. Historical names include Astraea undosa and Lithopoma undosum.

[, 11] - DerImb
The count of Dermasterias imbricata (leather star) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 12] - PisGig
The count of Pisaster giganteus (giant-spined sea star) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 13] - PycHel
The count of Pycnopodia helianthoides (sunflower sea star) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 14] - SteOsm
The count of Stephanocystis osmundacea (chainbladder kelp) individuals recorded on the transect. This species was formerly classified as Cystoseira osmundacea.

[, 15] - LamSpp
The count of kelp individuals identified to the genus Laminaria (Laminaria spp.) recorded on the transect.

[, 16] - MacJuv
The count of juvenile Macrocystis pyrifera (giant kelp) individuals less than 1 m tall recorded on the transect.

[, 17] - LamJuv
The count of juvenile kelp individuals in the order Laminariales for which the genus was uncertain, recorded on the transect.

[, 18] - EisArb
The count of Eisenia arborea (southern sea palm) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 19] - PteCal
The count of Pterygophora californica (woody-stemmed kelp) individuals recorded on the transect.

[, 20] - MacPyr
The count of Macrocystis pyrifera (giant kelp) individuals greater than 1 m tall recorded on the transect.
